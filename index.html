What's real

You will get better at these games with practice. That improvement is genuine neuroplasticity: repeated use strengthens the circuits a task depends on.

What's uncertain

Evidence that brain-game gains carry over to everyday thinking is limited. In 2014, about 70 scientists signed a consensus statement saying so. Rewire teaches the science; it makes no promise to raise IQ.

What it isn't

The map models your practice, not your brain, and Rewire is not a diagnostic tool. Progress is saved only in this browser.

About Rewire

Why I built Rewire

My name is Kellan Ismail, and I'm a premedical neuroscience student at Michigan State University! From a very young age, I've been fascinated by the idea of "use it or lose it," a core principle of what scientists call neuroplasticity. Your brain isn't locked into its ways: pathways can strengthen, bad habits can break, and with hard work you can keep getting better. My dream is to share this with everyone, young and old, and to help people understand that they aren't trapped in anything... not even their own brain!

Kellan Ismail
